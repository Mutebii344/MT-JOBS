Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MT Jobs

Job Seekers

How do I apply for a job?
Browse available jobs and click "Apply". Follow the instructions provided by the employer.
Do I need an account?
Yes, creating an account allows you to apply for jobs and track applications.

Employers

How do I post a job?
Sign up as an employer and click "Post a Job" from your dashboard.
Is posting jobs free?
Some listings may be free, while premium listings may require payment.

Account & Technical

I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page to reset your password.
How do I contact support?
You can reach us via the Contact page or email support@mtjobs.com.

Still need help?

Contact our support team for assistance.
